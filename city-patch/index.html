City Patch

Installation

Found object, chain, print on foambox, papier-mache, light, sound speaker

2025

What happens to a functional object when it no longer serves its function?

What does it become? Abandoned, displaced — or a simulation, an imitation of a “useful” thing?

Perhaps it becomes a performer, if the right music is playing. The noise of the city shifts into Shostakovich. Perhaps it becomes anything at all.


What’s the difference between degrees of the real — between an authentic object and a fake? Are some things only partially real, while others are somehow more real than real? And what is reality, exactly?

A hybrid reality, layered and unstable?
