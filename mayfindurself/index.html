Sculpture

100x200x100 cm

steel, screen, CCTV

2025

A CCTV camera pointed inside a rusted metal structure streams video and sound of a melting ice cube to a screen mounted on the outside.


With the interior completely blocked from view, the event exists solely as an electronic feed. This forces a suspicion: was the ice ever inside, or was the process staged entirely for the camera? Without a way to check inside, the question shifts: what is real, and how is reality transmitted?
