Reality is a Hack[9]

Installation, variable dimensions

Projection, computer graphics, digital printing on pvc

2023

Projection of the shadow of tree and window, alongside five explications of the work on significant and sharp topics: anti-capitalism, decolonialism, and the critique of anthropocentrism.


The work reproduces and parodies the situation of imitating and exploiting “grand narratives” in art. When any piece, regardless of its form, can be supplemented with an ethically sound and relevant interpretation, it transforms the artwork itself and its potential political impulse into a simulacrum, an echo. But still what is reality?
