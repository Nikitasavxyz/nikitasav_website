Shadow of Memory

Installation, variable dimensions

Projection, tree trunk, computer

2022

Behind the old sear tree trunk, the shadow of a mighty living tree is projected. The shadow is not realistic; it’s a little smudged, like memories.

One of the themes of the work is nostalgia. How accurate are these feelings, how much should we rely on them? Maybe our shadows were “cleared out” and presented in the best light, or perhaps they didn’t even exist?


This work is about former glory and illusions of it, about the life and death, being in between. The viewer is placed in an environment between the material dead and the non-material, but alive.


After all, shadow is the absence of light, and death is the absence of life.
