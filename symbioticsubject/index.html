Symbiotic Subject

Installation

150 x 170 x 150 cm

aluminium, bioplastic, hot glue

2023

Contrasting concepts, taking a specific position, we lose some, almost imperceptible, hidden part. We discard hundreds and thousands of possible options, therefore we get bogged down in contradictions. Nevertheless, it is precisely within that unexplored, intermediate, discarded, and transcendental territory that new perspectives and potential are hidden.


The work serves as a reminder that symbiotic relationships and solutions can only emerge when we are willing to break through the barriers of opposition. The work addresses to mycoremediation, a process in which fungi cleanse a given environment of toxic waste, including heavy metals, oil spills, and the effects of fires.

The artwork is a result of residence by Yemaa artspace in Atyrau, Kazakhstan.
