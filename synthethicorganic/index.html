Synthetic Organic

Digital art,

1920x3840, loop

3d software, computer

2020-2021

The project is dedicated to “how the artwork could look like when artists will take control” of organic material when we are able to program the physical properties and movements of organic structures.


The algorithmic, computerized process of synthesis something natural, and organic. Attempt to induce a meditative state, a sense of peace and tranquillity.
