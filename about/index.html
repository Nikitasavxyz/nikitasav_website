Nikita Savostyanov (b. 1997, Almaty) is a London-based artist working with sculpture and installation.


Within the framework of ‘Artificial Realism’, I create ‘physical renders,’ combining digital fabrication with analogue processes to translate the logic of 3D models into deceptive, hollow surfaces stripped of mass and function. These works play on the Victorian belief that mechanical processes could produce a reality more perfect than the original.


Placed alongside functional objects, these weightless simulations expose the slippage between solidity and construction, tracing our digital present back to a 19th-century obsession with the ‘authentic fake’.
